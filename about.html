Company profile

A premier Export, Import & Government Supplier, integrated under one roof.

Tahsin International is a premier Export, Import, and Government Supplier based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The firm is recognized as a First Class Contractor and holds a prestigious ABC-category Electrical Contractor License, authorizing it to undertake extra-high, medium, and low-pressure electrical installation works.

The company operates as a diverse conglomerate, providing a wide array of services including International Product Sourcing, Logistics and C&F services, Manpower Supply (security guards and caregivers), and Foreign Investment Joint Ventures. With a strong foundation in regulatory compliance, Tahsin International is a member of the Dhaka Chamber of Commerce & Industry (DCCI) and the Bangladesh Grocery Business Association.

The company is fully registered with a Value Added Tax (VAT) certificate, a Taxpayer's Identification Number (TIN), and an Import Registration Certificate (IRC) with an import slab of up to ৳5,00,00,000.
